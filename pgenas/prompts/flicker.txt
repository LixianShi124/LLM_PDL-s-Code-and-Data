Module 2: Physics-Informed Prior

The flicker branch receives a temporal feature sequence:

x_flick[n] = x_env[n] - 1.0

where x_env is the amplitude envelope obtained through TFM-FrZHAPE preprocessing.

The sequence contains 1200 samples, corresponding to a 200-ms observation window at 6 kHz.

Physical characteristics:
- Flicker is associated with low-frequency amplitude modulation.
- The generated flicker disturbances contain modulation frequencies in the range of 5–20 Hz.
- Modulation depth, temporal variation, and low-frequency fluctuation patterns provide useful identification information.

Architecture guidance:
- Learn modulation-related patterns from the envelope fluctuation sequence.
- Preserve relevant temporal dependencies across the observation window.
- Consider receptive fields capable of capturing relatively slow amplitude variations.
- Avoid aggressive early downsampling that may remove useful temporal information.
