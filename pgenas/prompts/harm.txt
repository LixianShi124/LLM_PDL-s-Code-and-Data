Module 2: Physics-Informed Prior

The harmonic branch receives a 604-dimensional feature vector.

Feature organization:
- Dimensions 1–600: FFT-based spectral amplitude features.
- Dimension 601: Total harmonic distortion (THD).
- Dimension 602: Third harmonic ratio (HR3).
- Dimension 603: Fifth harmonic ratio (HR5).
- Dimension 604: Seventh harmonic ratio (HR7).

The input therefore consists of 600 spectral components and four expert-defined statistical indicators.

Mandatory architectural constraint:

The four expert-defined statistical indicators must bypass all deep convolutional layers.

The architecture must:
- Extract learned representations from the 600-dimensional spectral component.
- Preserve the four expert-defined indicators through a separate pathway.
- Concatenate the expert indicators with the learned spectral features before the final classification head.

Do not mix the expert indicators into the convolutional spectral input.

Module 3: Resource-Aware Operator Guidance

For the spectral pathway, prefer efficient operators such as:
- Compact 1D convolutions with weight sharing.
- Depthwise separable convolutions.
- Lightweight bottleneck blocks.
- Adaptive pooling.
- Small MLP structures when appropriate.

Avoid:
- Large dense layers applied directly to flattened high-dimensional convolutional features.
- Excessive channel expansion.
- Unnecessary computational operations that increase FLOPs without improving feature extraction.

For the expert-feature pathway:
- Preserve all four statistical indicators.
- Use a direct bypass or lightweight transformation.
- Ensure that the expert pathway is integrated into the final classification stage.
