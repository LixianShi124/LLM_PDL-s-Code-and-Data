Module 2: Physics-Informed Prior

The transient branch receives two complementary temporal representations:

Channel 1: x_ori, the original voltage waveform.
Channel 2: r_res, the transient-enhanced residual.

Each channel contains 1200 temporal samples.

The residual is generated through:
1. Fundamental pre-removal:
   z[n] = x_ori[n] - x_fund[n]

2. Cycle-wise median subtraction:
   r_res[n] is reconstructed from the difference between z[n] and its cycle-wise median reference.

Physical characteristics:
- Impulsive transients contain short-duration abrupt waveform changes.
- Oscillatory transients contain localized damped oscillations.
- Voltage notches contain localized waveform depressions that may repeat at particular cycle positions.

Important architectural considerations:
- Preserve short-duration temporal details.
- Capture both localized transient changes and broader waveform context.
- Use the complementary information provided by the original waveform and residual.
- Avoid excessive early pooling that could suppress impulses or narrow notches.
- Support multiple temporal receptive fields when beneficial.
- Preserve information needed to identify damped oscillatory patterns.

Module 3: Resource-Aware Operator Guidance

Prefer:
- Compact 1D convolutional blocks.
- Multi-scale temporal kernels.
- Depthwise separable convolutions.
- Lightweight residual or bottleneck structures.
- Efficient attention modules when beneficial.
- Adaptive pooling with appropriate temporal feature preservation.

Avoid:
- Large dense layers on flattened temporal representations.
- Excessively wide convolutional blocks.
- Aggressive early temporal downsampling.
- Computationally expensive structures that exceed resource budgets.
